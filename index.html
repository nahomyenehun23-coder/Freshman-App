Freshman course · Human culture, diversity & identity

🧬 What is Anthropology?

Unit 1

Anthropology (from Greek anthropos + logos) – the study of humankind.

Scope: human origins, development, cultural variation, kinship, marriage, race, ethnicity, and more.

🔍 Holistic · Relativistic · Focused

  • Physical/Biological – human evolution, primatology, genetics
  • Archaeology – material remains: artefacts, features, eco‑facts
  • Linguistic – structural, historical, socio‑linguistics
  • Socio‑Cultural – ethnography, ethnology, society & culture
🔬 Ethnography = systematic study of individual cultures.
📊 Ethnology = comparative study of two or more cultures.

🌍 Culture & Ties

Unit 2

Culture – learned, shared, symbolic, all‑encompassing, integrated, adaptive/maladaptive, dynamic.

  • Enculturation – learning culture after birth.
  • Material culture – objects (tools, buildings).
  • Non‑material culture – values, beliefs, norms, language.
Folkways informal norms (e.g. offering seat).
Mores strict norms, severe sanctions.

Ethnocentrism – judging other cultures by own standards.
Cultural relativism – understanding behaviour within its own context.

Human rights supersede cultural practices when they harm individuals (e.g. FGM, slavery).

💍 Marriage & Kinship

Unit 2 (cont.)

Marriage rules: exogamy (outside group), endogamy (inside group).

  • Cross‑cousin – opposite‑sex siblings’ children.
  • Parallel‑cousin – same‑sex siblings’ children.
  • Levirate – widow marries husband’s brother.
  • Sororate – widower marries wife’s sister.

Polygyny (man, multiple wives) · Polyandry (woman, multiple husbands).

Economic aspects: Bride‑price, Bride‑service, Dowry.
Post‑marital residence: patrilocal, matrilocal, avunculocal, ambilocal, neolocal.

🌄 Human Diversity · Ethiopia

Unit 3

Humanity = Homo sapiens sapiens – bipedal, large brain, language, culture.

Cosmologies – traditional/religious origin stories.
Evolution – Darwinian: replication, variation, selection.

Cultural areas in Ethiopia:

  • Plough culture – highlands, agriculture.
  • Enset culture – south (Gurage, Sidama, Gedeo).
  • Pastoral culture – lowlands (Afar, Somali, Borena).
Biocultural evolution – biology and culture influence each other (e.g. brain size, diet, clothing).

⚖️ Marginalization

Unit 4

Marginalization – exclusion from social, economic, or political life.

  • Gender‑based – girls’ education, FGM, early marriage.
  • Occupational – tanners, potters, weavers, ironsmiths.
  • Age‑based – children, elderly.
Vulnerability – exposure to physical/emotional harm.
Minority groups – numerical or power minorities.

Human rights conventions condemn discrimination against women, children, disabled, elderly, and minorities.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Identity & Ethnicity

Unit 5

Ethnicity – shared descent, culture, language, religion, and solidarity.

  • Primordialism – ethnicity is innate, ancient, fixed.
  • Instrumentalism – ethnicity as a tool for political/economic goals.
  • Social constructivism – ethnicity is negotiated, flexible, situational.

Barth’s view: ethnicity is about social interaction and cultural differentiation – “us” vs “them”.

Race – a social construct; human genetic variation is minimal (>99% identical).
Historically divided into “Europeaus, Americanus, Asiaticus, Africanus” – now obsolete.

Ethnic identity – affiliative construct shaped by racial, natal, symbolic, and cultural factors.
